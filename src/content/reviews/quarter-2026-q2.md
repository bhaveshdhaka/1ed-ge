The hard quarter. The drawdown phase hit in May — six weeks of red, the tape
bleeding lower, and every recovery attempt getting sold. I reviewed the
numbers honestly: the edge didn't disappear, the process did. I was adding
size after losses and skipping the stop discipline I preach.

June was the repair — smaller, tighter, back to the plan. The quarter ends
net down but the June tape shows the climb rebuilding.

Lessons, written down so they stay: risk per trade is a ceiling, not a
suggestion. When the tape dips, the answer is smaller, not bigger.
