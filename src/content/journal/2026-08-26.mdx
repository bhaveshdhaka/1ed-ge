# the close call

Stopped out on a sloppy entry, and my hand literally started reaching for the next setup before my brain caught up. Recognized the exact feeling — clenched jaw, fast mouse, zero plan — and closed the laptop instead.

Logged the near-miss because near-misses are how the habit stays alive if you don't look at them. The bad version of today ends with a -2R revenge trade. The real version ends with a long walk and a clear head.
