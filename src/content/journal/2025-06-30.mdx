The numbers were fine. That was the problem. A few good weeks in, I started sizing up after winners — the same habit the q2 review flagged. The tape bled, and instead of stopping I added. The drawdown limit was there for a reason; I watched it get hit.

What I'll do different: risk per trade is a ceiling, not a suggestion. When the tape dips, the answer is smaller, not bigger.
