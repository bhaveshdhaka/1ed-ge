# from five trades to two

I used to think volume was effort. Five trades a day, always in something, always explaining myself in the journal afterward.

Cut to a max of two A setups a day. Same risk per trade. The quality of my entries went up, my losses got smaller, and +10.9R last month beat any month I was 'busy'.

The journal has the receipts. Fewer, better, logged.
